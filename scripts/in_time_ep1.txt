Title: In Time - Episode 1: The Clock Starts

INT. TIME BANK - DAY

The interior of a sterile, futuristic TIME BANK. Digital clocks line the walls, each ticking down. The air feels clinical, cold.

WILL SALAS, a tall, lean man in his late 20s with sharp features and determined eyes, wearing a worn leather jacket, stands at a counter. His left forearm glows green: 23:14:07 — less than a day to live.

WILL
I need to withdraw three hours.

TELLER
Minimum withdrawal is one day, sir.

WILL
I don't have a day to spare.

Behind Will, a wealthy man in a tailored suit walks in. This is HENRY LEON, age 105 but looking 25. His forearm reads: 9847:22:15 — over a century of time.

HENRY
Give him what he needs.

Will turns, suspicious.

EXT. NEW LOS ANGELES - DAY

A sprawling metropolis where everything costs time. Billboards read "TIME IS MONEY — LITERALLY." Flying cars zip between glass towers. A massive countdown clock towers over the city center.

Will walks through crowded streets where people rush everywhere — nobody can afford to be slow. Street vendors sell coffee for 4 minutes, a ride on the transit pod costs 15 minutes.

EXT. BRIDGE - NIGHT

Will and Henry stand on a bridge overlooking the city lights. Henry looks tired — not physically, but existentially.

HENRY
I'm tired of living, Will. I've had a century and I've done nothing with it. The system is designed to keep people dying so the wealthy can live forever.

WILL
Then why give me your time?

HENRY
Because you'll do something with it. You'll fight.

Henry transfers his time to Will. Will's forearm flashes: 9870:36:22. Henry collapses, his clock hitting zero.

A TIME KEEPER patrol car screams past in the distance.

INT. TIME KEEPER HEADQUARTERS - NIGHT

A dark, militaristic command center. Screens show city surveillance. 

TIMEKEEPER RAYMOND LEON, a grizzled man in a black uniform with silver badges, studies a screen showing Henry's final moments.

RAYMOND
Find the man who took Henry Leon's time. Nobody just gives away a century.

A junior keeper, JAIME, pulls up Will's file.

JAIME
Will Salas. Dayton sector. 23 hours when he entered the bank. Now he has over a century.

RAYMOND
That's our man.

EXT. GHETTO STREETS - NIGHT

Will runs through the dark, narrow streets of the Dayton ghetto. He's rich for the first time in his life — and terrified. He passes a wall of memorial photos: people who've "timed out."

He stops at a small apartment. Knocks. 

SYLVIA WEIS, a striking woman in her 20s with dark hair and intelligent eyes, opens the door. She's the daughter of a time-lending tycoon, but she hates the system.

SYLVIA
You're the one they're looking for.

WILL
I need to disappear. Can you help?

She studies his forearm — the glowing century of time.

SYLVIA
You're the richest man in Dayton. Come in.

INT. SYLVIA'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Sylvia's apartment is modest but filled with art and books — contraband in a world where every second counts.

SYLVIA
My father owns half the time banks. He'd kill to meet you — or kill you, depending on what you plan to do with that time.

WILL
I plan to change things.

EXT. CITY - DAWN

The sun rises over New Los Angeles. The countdown clock at city center resets: 24:00:00 for the wealthy. In the ghetto below, people hurry to work, clocks blinking red.

Will and Sylvia walk together toward the transit hub. A TIME KEEPER drone hovers above, scanning faces.

SYLVIA
There's a time vault downtown. My father's. If we could redistribute even a fraction of what's in there...

WILL
How much is in it?

SYLVIA
Enough to give everyone in Dayton a year.

They board a sleek TRANSIT POD that shoots through a glass tube toward the city center.

INT. TIME VAULT - DAY

A massive underground vault filled with glowing time capsules. Each capsule holds decades, centuries of stored time. Security cameras everywhere.

Will and Sylvia enter through a service entrance. Sylvia uses her father's access code.

The vault door opens. The light inside is almost blinding — rows and rows of time, enough to save millions.

SYLVIA
This is what immortality looks like when hoarded.

Suddenly — alarms. Red lights. TIMEKEEPERS pour in from every exit.

RAYMOND (O.S.)
Will Salas! Freeze!

Will grabs Sylvia's hand. They run.

EXT. CITY STREETS - DAY

A chase through the crowded streets. Will and Sylvia dodge flying cars and pedestrians. Time Keeper drones dive after them.

They reach the bridge where Henry died. Will looks at his forearm — he's spent 2 days already. 9868:14:33.

WILL
We have to get the time out. To the people.

SYLVIA
How?

Will looks at the countdown clock towering over the city.

WILL
We crash the clock.

FADE OUT.

END OF EPISODE 1
